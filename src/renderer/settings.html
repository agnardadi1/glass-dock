Auto-hide

When a full-screen app or a game is in front (Steam, Epic, Battle.net, Riot, GOG, Xbox, EA, Ubisoft, a Games folder, or anything Windows knows as a game), the dock stays hidden until you Alt + Tab out or close the game, and the island goes quiet. Right-click an icon on the dock and tick "Treat as a game" for anything it misses.
